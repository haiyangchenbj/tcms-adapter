## Description:

Adapts reviewed marketing drafts and published articles into channel-specific content and re-promotion materials.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[haiyangchenbj](https://clawhub.ai/user/haiyangchenbj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Tech-product marketing teams adapt an already reviewed core draft or published article into WeChat, developer-community, social, LinkedIn, and sales materials for human review before publication.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Sensitive details or unreviewed claims in a source draft may carry into channel copies.

Mitigation: Use a reviewed, redacted source and check claims and customer details in every adapted version before publishing.

Risk: Broad trigger phrases and default channel or language choices may produce unintended drafts.

Mitigation: Confirm the target channels and languages before generation, then review each draft for its intended audience.
